About the Author

Alpha Amadu Jalloh

Author · Columnist · Human Rights Advocate · Peacebuilder · Public Commentator

Alpha Amadu Jalloh writes from Sierra Leone about the questions many are too careful to ask out loud, about power, inequality, leadership and the everyday humanity that connects people across politics. His work spans books, essays, editorials and public commentary, united under one identity: The Misfit Voice.

2026Nairobi Book Launch

A citizen who insists on asking

At the center of Alpha's writing is a simple conviction: questioning leadership is not an act of hostility. It is part of citizenship. That belief runs through Mr. President, Can We Talk?, launched in Nairobi in August 2026 with Professor P. L. O. Lumumba officiating.

It was there before that book, too, in Monopoly of Happiness, his earlier examination of who in Sierra Leone gets to enjoy opportunity, and who is left outside it.

Beyond politics, into people

Essays like Everyone Is a Relative turn toward ordinary life: relationships, morality, dignity and the quiet things that connect strangers into something like family.

And in I Am a Misfit No One Can Tame, he turns the question on himself: what does it cost, and what does it earn, to refuse the shape everyone else expects you to take?

What He Stands For

Four threads through the work

Accountability

Holding power to the same standard it asks of citizens.

Equity

Naming who is included, and who has been left out.

Peace

Building the conditions for peace, not just its appearance.

Human Dignity

Writing that treats every person's story as worth telling.

Question Power. Question Society. Question Yourself.
