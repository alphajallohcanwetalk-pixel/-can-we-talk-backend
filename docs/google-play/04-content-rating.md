## Note on the political content

Alpha's work is political commentary and social criticism: leadership,
accountability, inequality, governance. That is legitimate published work by a
named author, not hate speech, and it does not require a mature rating on its
own. Do not try to hide it, and do not overstate it either. If a reviewer asks,
the answer is that this is an author's published non-fiction.
