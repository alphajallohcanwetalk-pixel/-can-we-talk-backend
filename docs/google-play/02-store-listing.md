## Full description (4000 max)

```
Can We Talk? is the home of Alpha Amadu Jalloh, the Sierra Leonean author,
columnist, human rights advocate and peacebuilder known as The Misfit Voice.

Question Power. Question Society. Question Yourself.

WHAT YOU WILL FIND

Books
Read about and order Alpha's published work, including Mr. President, Can We
Talk? A Citizen's Conversation with Power, launched in Nairobi with Professor
P. L. O. Lumumba, and Monopoly of Happiness, his examination of inequality and
who in Sierra Leone gets to enjoy opportunity.

The Essay Library
Years of essays and editorials in one searchable place, on leadership, justice,
democracy, inequality, migration, and the ordinary human questions underneath
all of them.

A proper reading experience
Books and essays open as a real book, two pages at a time, turning page by page,
with adjustable type. Pages you have already opened stay readable offline.

Order signed copies
Buy paperback and hardcover editions and request the author's signature.
Secure checkout, with your order history kept in your account.

Follow the work
Events, launch galleries, press coverage and speaking enquiries.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Alpha Amadu Jalloh writes from Sierra Leone about the questions many are too
careful to ask out loud: about power, inequality, leadership and the everyday
humanity that connects people across politics. His conviction is simple, that
questioning leadership is not hostility but citizenship.

SUPPORT

questions@canwetalkvoice.com
```

## App icon

512 x 512 PNG, 32-bit, no transparency, no rounded corners (Play applies its
own mask). See `07-app-icon.md`.

## Feature graphic (required)

1024 x 500 PNG or JPEG. No transparency. Keep text well inside the middle, as
the edges get cropped on some surfaces.

## Screenshots

Phone screenshots are required: between 2 and 8, each 16:9 or 9:16, with the
shortest side at least 320px and the longest at most 3840px.

Suggested set, in this order:
1. Home, with the hero and book covers
2. A book page showing price and format options
3. The reader open at a two page spread
4. The Essay Library with the search and category filters
5. The about page

Take them on a real device so the layout is honest. Do not add fake review
counts, fake ratings or claims the app does not support: Play rejects listings
whose screenshots misrepresent the app.
